# Design Review

## Low-fidelity sketches

📄 [View the sketch (PDF)](./Webdesignsketch.pdf)

The sketch I drew identifies the key elements of the UX of the website, and also I wrote the keyfeatures I wanted.

## List of taken considerations for sketches/design

1. **User flow clarity** – Ensure the user can go from browsing deals to identifying valuable ones quickly.
2. **Responsiveness** – The layout should adapt nicely for desktop and mobile views.
3. **Focus on filters** – Filters were positioned and highlighted early to encourage user engagement.

## Main problem we are solving for

The main problem is that users interested in LEGO deals often struggle to identify the best discounts, the different platforms that sell the your favorite items!. 
This interface aims to **simplify decision-making** by offering sorting, filtering, and visual indicators, as well as deals and Vinted finds so that you pay the best price for your favorite lego set.

## Measures of the success

Success will be measured by:

- How quickly a user can identify the best deal.
- Whether users can use filters and sorters intuitively.
- If the interface helps differentiate between normal, hot, and favorite deals.

## List of visual aspects convictions

1. **Layout** – Clear separation of controls (`filters`, `sort`, `pagination`) and results (`deals`, `indicators`, `sales`).
2. **Color choice** – Warm yellow-orange tones create a playful atmosphere, consistent with LEGO's fun branding.
3. **Font choice** – A rounded font was chosen (Comic sans MS) to enhance the friendly mood of the page, and gives a retro vibe, coherent with some old deals.
4. **Spacing** – Adequate padding between cards and controls prevents clutter and makes navigation and focus easier.
5. **Rounded edges** - Rounded edges for buttons and categories to have a clean output

## List of interaction patterns convictions

1. **Button interactions** – Filters are displayed as **pill-style buttons** with hover and active states for clarity.
2. **Dropdowns for pagination/sorting** – Simple `select` elements are used for accessibility and quick changes.
3. **Favorite feature** – Uses a clear `Save as favorite` button per deal, and a corresponding filter button to retrieve them.
